Cancellation & Refund Policy

Deposit
A 50% deposit is required at the time of booking to secure your ride.

Remaining Balance
The remaining balance must be paid no later than 72 hours before your scheduled pickup time. A reminder will be sent at 96 hours out giving you 24 hours to complete payment.

Non-Payment
If the remaining balance is not received by 72 hours before pickup, Group Ride KC reserves the right to cancel the ride and retain the deposit.

Cancellations — More Than 72 Hours Out
If you cancel more than 72 hours before your pickup time, you will receive a 50% refund of all payments made.

Cancellations — Within 72 Hours
Cancellations made within 72 hours of your scheduled pickup are non-refundable.

Rescheduling
Need to change your date or time? No problem — reschedule your ride at any time and your deposit will carry over to your new booking.
